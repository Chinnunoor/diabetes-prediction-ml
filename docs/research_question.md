# Research question

## Central question

How can machine-learning models provide accurate and interpretable early diabetes risk prediction using clinical attributes?

## Healthcare AI framing

This project treats diabetes outcome prediction as a binary supervised-learning problem. The intended research contribution is not simply to maximize accuracy, but to examine the relationship between predictive performance, class-imbalance handling, feature selection, and interpretability.

The repository is an exploratory research portfolio. Its results should not be interpreted as clinical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or evidence that a model is ready for deployment. A clinically meaningful study would require a clearly documented cohort, clinically validated labels, external evaluation, calibration analysis, subgroup assessment, and prospective workflow evaluation.

## Research objectives

1. Establish transparent baseline performance for several common classifiers.
2. Examine how SMOTE and feature-selection experiments affect recorded performance.
3. Identify candidate features for further explainability analysis.
4. Document limitations that matter when translating tabular ML research into healthcare settings.
